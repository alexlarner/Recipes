# Chocolate

## Ingredients

- 170 grams (3/4 cup) **coconut oil**, unrefined
- 72 grams (3/4 cup) **cacao powder**
- 177 (1/2 cup and 1 tablespoon) **maple syrup**
- 3/8 teaspoon **salt**
- Coarse sea salt (for garnish)

### Orange

Add 4 grams (1 1/2 teaspoons) **orange zest**, dried and powdered

## Directions

1. Melt the coconut oil
1. Add the cacao powder, salt, and other seasonings (if applicable) and stir until combined
1. Let sit for 4 minutes
1. Add the maple syrup and stir until combined & smooth
1. Pour into molds
1. Freeze for at least 30 minutes or put in fridge for at least 4 hours
