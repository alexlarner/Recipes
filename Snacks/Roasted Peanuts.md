# Roasted Peanuts

## Ingredients

- 584 grams (4 cups) **peanuts**, raw
- 12 grams (2 teaspoons) **salt**

## Honey/Maple Roasted Peanuts

Add:

- (85 grams) 1/4 cup **honey**
    - Or 79 grams (1/4 cup) **maple syrup**

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350 F
1. Cook peanuts for 20 minutes, stirring halfway through
1. Take peanuts right out of the oven and dump them into a bowl
1. If you're making honey/maple roasted peanuts, pour the honey/maple syrup right over the hot peanuts and stir
1. Sprinkle the salt over the peanuts and stir
1. If you're making honey/maple roasted peanuts, give them a stir every 5 minutes for 30 minutes or until cooled
