# Steak Seasoning

*Makes 3 1/3 cups*

## Ingredients

- 109 grams (1 cup) **paprika**
- 72 grams (1/2 cup) **black peppercorns**
- 148 grams (1/2 cup) **salt**
- 77 grams (1/2 cup) **garlic powder**
- 48 grams (1/2 cup) **ground coriander**
- 3.5 grams (4 teaspoons) **thyme**
- 2.25 grams (4 teaspoons) **marjoram**

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350 F
1. Toast coriander & peppercorns in the oven for 8 minutes
1. Let toasted spices cool to room temperature
1. Grind everything to a powder, except for the paprika, garlic powder, and salt
1. Combine all ingredients and mix well
